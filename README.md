**GRC Analyst | CompTIA Security+ | Lagos, Nigeria (Remote)**

I deliver live compliance engagements for fintech and banking clients across **PCI-DSS, SOC 2, ISO 27001/27017/27018 and NDPA**: preparing SAQs and AOCs, mapping policies to controls, auditing evidence, and guiding clients through gap remediation. I back that work with hands-on security practice in vulnerability assessment, SIEM monitoring, incident response and threat intelligence, so my findings are grounded in how controls actually work.

## 🎯 Objective

To grow as a GRC and cybersecurity specialist who understands both sides of security: what a framework requires, and what is happening on the systems it is meant to protect. I want to help organisations close real gaps, not just pass audits, and to explain risk in plain language that technical and non-technical teams can act on.
